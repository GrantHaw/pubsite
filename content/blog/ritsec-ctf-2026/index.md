Here are my writeups for the RITSEC CTF 2026! Here, we got first place out of all RIT teams. I never really had a good place to post these but now i have myself a pretty little blog >:) These are NOT going to be the most... comprehensive or professional writeups. I made them during the competition (when the wounds were fresh) so I would remember how to complete each challenge.

Overall, this was a super fun competition. It was nice to be forced to actually figure stuff out myself rather than turn them in to AI and submit the flag. 

My favs were Monitor Breaker, average contrived notes app, Pirate's Lost Log and Song Inside a Shell.
